# Portal 0.2.0

Adds an **Accurate** mode for maps that stay closer to the real place.

## New: Detail

Under the size buttons you now choose **Standard** (as before) or
**Accurate (slower)**:

- Real building outlines and heights from Microsoft's and Overture's building
  data wherever OpenStreetMap has none, and every mapped building is kept.
- No invented blocks of flats. Choose the building style for buildings with no
  data, American (mostly houses) or European, or let Portal guess from the
  country. In Bardstown, Kentucky, flats went from 43% of the buildings to 4.6%.
- Buildings can be up to 12 storeys instead of 6, so surveyed heights aren't cut.
- **Roads follow their real curves** (on by default): roads use the game's 16
  directions and stay close to their real line instead of being straightened.
  This is the slow part, about an hour for a Town and hours for a City core.
  Switch it off to keep Standard's roads and still get the building fixes.
- An Accurate build is its own mod, e.g. "Bardstown, Kentucky (Town, accurate)",
  so it never replaces a Standard one.
- If the extra building data can't be downloaded, Portal says so instead of
  quietly making a Standard map.

Measured on the developer's PC, Bardstown (Town): Standard about 16 minutes,
Accurate with real-curve roads 95 minutes. Details in
[docs/accurate-mode.md](../accurate-mode.md).

## You need

- Windows, Project Zomboid on Build 42.
- [KnoxMap](https://github.com/spytheeuclidean-a11y/knoxmap) 1.8.x (tested
  with 1.8.4), installed separately with its `Setup.bat`.

## Notes

- Enable only one Portal town at a time: they are all placed at the same spot in
  the world.
- Accurate mode has been compared with Standard on one town; it has not yet
  been played in the game. Multiplayer has not been tested.
- `Portal.exe` is not code-signed. SmartScreen may warn: **More info > Run
  anyway**.
